          The Science Behind Action

          • 🎯
            MIT / Eat the Frog: One chosen priority beats long lists. Your brain clears when the hardest thing is done first.
          • 📅
            Implementation Intentions (Gollwitzer): "I will do X at time Y in place Z" increases follow-through by 2–3×.
          • ⏱️
            Pomodoro / Time-boxing: Bounded sprints lower activation energy. The hardest part is starting.
          • 🔁
            Zeigarnik Effect: Open loops occupy attention. Focus mode shows one task at a time to exploit the pull to close it.
          • 🧊
            Soft Streaks: Loss aversion motivates, but hard streaks cause quitting. One forgiveness day per week keeps momentum alive.
          • ⚡
            2-Minute Rule (Allen): If it takes less than 2 minutes, do it now — scheduling it costs more than doing it.
          • 📊
            Eisenhower Matrix: Urgency feels important but rarely is. Separate the two to spend time on what actually matters.
          • 🎉
            Progress Principle (Amabile): Visible small wins are the top daily motivator. Every completion matters.
